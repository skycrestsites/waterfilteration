02Online pricing

HomeForm operates online. We do not send salespeople to your home or perform in-home water testing. If we find conditions at installation that change the scope of work, we will explain them and confirm any change with you before work begins.

03Quotes and pricing

  • Every quote is provided in writing and is valid for 30 days.
  • All published prices are out-the-door prices: equipment, standard installation and fees included. Standard installation covers connection to accessible plumbing near the main water line, a bypass valve, drain line and leak check.
  • Non-standard work, such as relocating plumbing, electrical work, drain installation or permit fees, is identified and priced in writing before any work begins.
  • Prices may change for future quotes, but never after you've accepted a written quote.
