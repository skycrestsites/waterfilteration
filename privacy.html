01Who we are

Clearwell Water Co. LLC ("Clearwell," "we," "us") installs and services residential water treatment systems, including water softeners, whole-home filtration, reverse osmosis and well water treatment, in Greater Springfield and surrounding communities.

This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through our website, phone calls, text messages and in-home appointments. By using our website or contacting us, you agree to the practices described here.

02Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details such as your name, phone number, email address, street address and ZIP code.
  • Home and water details such as your water source (city or well), the problems you're noticing, household size and plumbing notes.
  • Water test results recorded during your free in-home test.
  • Payment and financing details when you purchase a system. Card payments are processed by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser type, IP address, pages visited, referring website and time spent on the site.
  • Information from cookies and similar technologies, described in Cookies and analytics.

03How we use your information

  • To respond to your request and schedule your free water test.
  • To prepare a written recommendation and price for your home.
  • To install, maintain and service your system, and to honor your warranty.
  • To send appointment confirmations, reminders and filter or salt reminders.
  • To improve our website, services and customer experience.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

04Calls and text messages

If you check the consent box on our form or text us first, you agree to receive calls and text messages from Clearwell Water Co. about your request, appointments and service. Message frequency varies, and is typically no more than 4 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply.

Your mobile information is never sold. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out. You'll receive one final confirmation message.
  • Reply HELP for help, or call (555) 214-7700.
  • Consent to receive texts is not a condition of receiving a free water test or purchasing any system.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

05Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run our business, and only as needed to do their job:

Provider typePurpose
CRM and schedulingStoring your request, booking appointments and sending reminders.
Website hosting and formsRunning this website and receiving form submissions.
Payment and financingProcessing payments and financing applications you choose to submit.
Equipment manufacturersRegistering your system so manufacturer warranties apply.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or safety, or as part of a merger or sale of our business.

06Cookies and analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working, understand how visitors use it and measure our advertising. These may include Google Analytics and, when advertising is active, the Google Ads and Meta pixels.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can also opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on, and manage ad preferences in your Google and Meta account settings. Fonts on this site load from Google Fonts.

07Data retention

We keep customer records for as long as you own a system we installed, so we can honor your warranty and service history, and as required for tax and legal purposes. Leads who do not become customers are deleted after 24 months unless you ask us to remove them sooner.

08Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls and reputable service providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work hard to protect your data.

09Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: ask for a copy of the information we hold about you or ask us to correct it.
  • Deletion: ask us to delete your information, subject to legal and warranty record requirements.
  • Marketing: unsubscribe from emails using the link in any message, or reply STOP to texts.
  • State rights: depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws. We will honor these requests as required.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

10Children's privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the effective date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be highlighted on our website.
