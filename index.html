Why don't you do in-home sales visits?

In-home sales visits are long, high-pressure and expensive, and customers end up paying for them through higher prices. We talk with you over the phone instead, recommend what fits your home and budget, and give you one honest, out-the-door price. Then our team comes out to install it.

What does "out the door" price mean?

It means the price you see is the total you pay: the equipment, standard installation and all fees. No surprise add-ons on install day.

Do you come out to test my water?

No. Skipping the in-home sales visit is a big part of how we keep prices low. Tell us whether you're on city water or a well and what you're noticing, and we'll recommend the right system. If you already have a water report, send it over and we'll factor it in.

How do I know which system I need?

Most homes on Cincinnati city water get the biggest improvement from a softener with carbon filtration, and many add a drinking water system. Homes on private wells with staining or a sulfur smell usually need our well water system. Not sure? Choose "help me choose" on the form and we'll guide you.

Who installs the system?

Our professional installation team. We install, test for leaks, set up the system for your home and walk you through how it works before we leave.

What equipment do you use?

American-made systems assembled in Fort Worth, Texas, built on a ceramic disc control valve with a 72-hour memory backup. They come with lifetime coverage on tanks and valve wear parts, 5 years on electronics and 12 months on media.
