Sound familiar?

Your water tells you when something is wrong.

Most of the problems homeowners call us about come down to a handful of causes. Each one has a specific fix, and we only recommend the one your water needs.

White spots and scale

Crusty faucets, cloudy glasses and a water heater that works harder every year. That is hard water minerals building up.

Fix: Whole-home softener

Dry skin and dull hair

Hard water and chlorine strip natural oils. Soap does not lather and leaves a film on skin, hair and tile.

Fix: Softening plus carbon filtration

Rust and orange staining

Orange rings in toilets, stained laundry and fixtures. Common with private wells that carry iron and manganese.

Fix: Iron filtration system

Rotten egg smell

A sulfur odor from hot or cold taps usually points to hydrogen sulfide in well water. It is treatable.

Fix: Air injection oxidation

Chlorine taste or smell

City water is disinfected with chlorine or chloramine. Safe, yes, but it shows up in your coffee, ice and shower.

Fix: Whole-home carbon filtration

Appliances wearing out

Scale shortens the life of water heaters, dishwashers and washing machines, and quietly raises your energy bill.

Fix: Softener sized to your home

Know your water

City water and well water need different fixes.

We start every recommendation by testing what actually comes out of your tap, not by guessing from a brochure.

City water

Treated before it reaches you, and you can taste it.

Municipal water is safe to drink but often hard and heavily chlorinated. Over time the minerals build up in your plumbing and appliances.

HardnessChlorineChloramineScale buildupTaste & odor
Usually recommended: whole-home softener with carbon filtration, plus reverse osmosis at the kitchen sink.

Private well

Untreated, and different at every address.

Well water comes straight from the ground. Two homes on the same street can have completely different water, so testing comes first, always.

Is the water test really free?

Yes. The in-home test takes about 20 minutes, there is no charge and no obligation. We write the results down and leave them with you whether you buy anything or not.

How long does installation take?

Most whole-home systems are installed in 3 to 5 hours. Well water systems can take a full day. Your water is only shut off for a short part of that time.

Do you rent systems, or do I own it?

You own it from day one. We do not rent equipment and we never require a monthly service contract.

Will a water softener make my water taste salty?

No. Softeners exchange hardness minerals for a very small amount of sodium that most people cannot taste. Adding a reverse osmosis system at the kitchen sink removes it from your drinking water entirely.

How much maintenance is involved?

Very little. Add salt to the brine tank every 6 to 8 weeks and change your RO filters roughly once a year. We send reminders and offer an optional annual service visit.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. We offer 0% APR financing on approved credit, with simple monthly payments. We always quote your total price first so you can compare apples to apples.

Will a softener lower my water pressure?

Not when it's sized correctly. Our valves are rated up to 28 gallons per minute, which comfortably covers multiple showers and appliances running at once.
