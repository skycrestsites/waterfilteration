Why are you 100% online?

In-home sales visits are expensive, and customers end up paying for them through higher prices. By running online, we keep our costs low and pass that savings directly to you. You still get a real person on the phone and professional installation.

What does "out the door" price mean?

It means the price you see is the total you pay: the equipment, standard installation and all fees. No surprise add-ons on install day.

Do you come out to test my water?

No. Skipping the in-home visit is a big part of how we keep prices low. Tell us whether you're on city water or a well and what you're noticing, and we'll recommend the right system. If you already have a water report, send it over and we'll factor it in.

How do I know which system I need?

Most homes on Cincinnati city water get the biggest improvement from a water softener, and many add a drinking water system. Homes on private wells with staining or a sulfur smell usually need an iron and sulfur filter first. Not sure? Choose "help me choose" on the form and we'll guide you.

Who installs the system?

Our professional installation team. We install, test for leaks, set up the system for your home and walk you through how it works before we leave.

What equipment do you use?

We use equipment from Nelsen Corporation, a water treatment supplier headquartered in Medina, Ohio. Their systems are built for residential use and backed by a manufacturer warranty.
