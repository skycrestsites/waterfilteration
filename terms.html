01Acceptance of terms

These terms apply to your use of this website and to services provided by Clearwell Water Co. LLC ("Clearwell," "we," "us"). By using the site, submitting a form or scheduling a service, you agree to these terms and to our Privacy Policy.

02Free water tests

Our in-home water test is free and carries no obligation to buy. Results are measured on site with professional test equipment and are provided to help recommend treatment. They are not a certified laboratory analysis. If you need certified results for health, lending or real estate purposes, we can refer you to an accredited lab.

03Quotes and pricing

  • Every quote is provided in writing and is valid for 30 days.
  • Published package prices include standard installation. Standard installation covers connection to accessible plumbing near the main water line, a bypass valve, drain line and pressure test.
  • Non-standard work, such as relocating plumbing, electrical work, drain installation or permit fees, is identified and priced in writing before any work begins.
  • Prices may change for future quotes, but never after you've accepted a written quote.

Installations are performed by licensed plumbers. You agree to provide reasonable access to the installation area and a working electrical outlet where required. We'll pull permits when local code requires them. Your water may be shut off for a short time during installation.
